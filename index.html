WHO WE ARE

Building financial confidence, one young person at a time.

M

“Our goal is to make financial education feel practical, approachable, and relevant to young people.”

Money Mind is a youth-led organization created to help young people understand money and feel more confident making financial decisions. We focus on useful knowledge that can connect to real life — from budgeting and saving to financial goal-setting and understanding everyday money choices.

Through education, workshops, outreach, events, and youth-led projects, we want to create a community where asking questions about money is encouraged and learning about finance is accessible.
